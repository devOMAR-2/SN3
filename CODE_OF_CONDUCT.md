# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment.

## Expected behavior
Be constructive, assume good faith, respect different viewpoints, and give actionable feedback. Protect other people's privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized misconduct, personal attacks, or publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through an appropriate private contact channel on their GitHub profile. **Do not publish sensitive reports in public Issues.** Maintainers will review reports fairly and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants.

## Scope
This policy applies to repository discussions, Issues, Pull Requests, and official project community spaces.

Adapted from widely used open-source community conduct principles. This document is a project-specific policy.
